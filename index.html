Most plans ignore your season

The problem

Athletes want a higher vertical, but most programs are fixed 8 to 12 week PDFs that do not know when you play. Paid apps hide the useful parts behind a subscription. Coaching that gives real feedback runs $40 to $100 a session. Many players end up hurt: ankles at the net, knees on landings, tendons from overuse.

The solution

Vert Plan is a free plan that puts strength first and builds jumping on top of it. You enter your games and practices, and the week re-sorts around them. After each workout you log how your body feels, and a painful week gets lighter. You can film a set and compare it with a coach demo to check your own form.

Three things the MVP does

A week that moves around games

Set your game and practice days. Heavy leg work and jump sessions shift away from game days, and a load bar warns you when the week is packed.

Strength first, jumps second

Squats, hinges, single-leg work and calves build the base. Ankle and knee prep starts every session. Plyometrics and footwork come on their own day.

Film a set, check your form

Record a clip, watch it next to a coach demo, and answer a short checklist like "Did your knees cave in?". You get one clear cue to work on and a reminder to re-check.
